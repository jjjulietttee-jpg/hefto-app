Common questions

I paid but Pro is locked.

Open Settings (top corner of the app), find the Hefto Pro section and tap Restore purchases. Be on the same Apple ID that bought it. If the message says no active subscription was found, mail us a screenshot of the Apple receipt.

How do I cancel?

Settings on your iPhone, your name, Subscriptions, Hefto, Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.

My route has a straight line or gaps.

That is a lost GPS signal. Set location access to While Using for Hefto, keep Precise Location on, and avoid stuffing the phone deep inside a pack with Low Power Mode on. Starting the ruck from your Watch uses the Watch GPS instead.

The Watch app shows nothing.

The Watch app needs a watchOS install alongside the iPhone one. Check the Watch app on your iPhone, confirm Hefto is listed under Available Apps, and reopen both.

Calories seem low or high.

Update Body weight and Pack in Settings. The result uses the Pandolf formula with your speed, slope and ground, so a wrong pack weight skews it directly.

Rucks are not appearing in Apple Health.

Turn on Save rucks to Apple Health in Settings and allow workouts, active energy and walking distance writing when iOS asks. Past rucks are not back-filled.

Can I delete my data?

Swipe a ruck in the Log and tap Delete, or delete the app to wipe everything stored on the phone. For the Adapty record, send us an email.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your Pro status follows your Apple ID.
