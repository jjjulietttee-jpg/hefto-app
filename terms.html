Hefto is a training log, not a doctor, a coach, or a guide to the backcountry. Walk loaded at your own pace and judgment.

1. Be safe out there

Carrying weight puts strain on knees, hips, feet and spine. Build up slowly, and see a clinician first if you have a heart, joint or back condition, are pregnant, or have not trained for a while. Calories are an estimate made from your weight, your load, pace, slope and ground type. They are not a clinical measurement and can be off by a good margin. Maps, routes and distances come from the phone's GPS and can drift in canyons, forests and cities. Plan a route and carry water independent of this app.
