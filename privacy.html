Privacy

Revised October 6, 2026

Short version: your routes, pack weights and heart-rate numbers stay on your own devices. The only outside service in the app is Adapty, which looks after Pro purchases.

GPS on the trail

Hefto asks for location only when you are using the app. Once you start a ruck it keeps recording with the screen locked, then stops the moment you finish. It samples roughly every three meters of movement. While a ruck is running, a small recovery file is written to the app's private folder so a crash or a phone restart does not eat your walk. We never see any of these points, and the app has no map-tile or routing service attached to it.

Apple Watch

If you start a ruck from your wrist, the Watch app records the workout session itself, using the Watch's GPS and heart sensor, and hands the result to the iPhone over Apple's built-in Watch link. No third party sits in that path.

Apple Health

The switch is called Save rucks to Apple Health in Settings and starts off. When on, Hefto adds a walking workout with distance and active energy. The iPhone app does not read your Health records. You can pull permission back anytime in the Health app under Sharing, then Apps.

Purchases and Adapty

Pro runs through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple takes your payment details; Hefto never sees a card number. Adapty, a subscription-infrastructure company, receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous profile ID, your device model and OS version, your approximate country as inferred from the App Store storefront, and a few counters such as how far you got in onboarding. It uses them to tell the app whether you have Pro. Adapty publishes its own policy at adapty.io.

We do not use advertising SDKs, we do not follow you across other companies' apps, and we do not sell anything about you.

Sending us feedback

The Send feedback button opens a pre-addressed email in your mail app. It pre-fills the app version, your iOS version and your Adapty profile ID so we can find the right purchase record. You can delete those lines before sending.

Clearing it out

  • One walk: in the Log tab, swipe it and tap Delete.
  • Everything on the phone: delete Hefto from your home screen; the routes and settings go with it.
  • Workouts already placed in Apple Health: remove them in the Health app.
  • Adapty's copy: email us from the address in the footer and we will have the profile erased.

Who this is for

Hefto is a tool for adults and teens. A child younger than 13 should not use it, and if we learn one has, the Adapty record gets erased on request.

If this page changes

A revision gets a new date at the top. Larger shifts will be mentioned inside the app's release notes.
